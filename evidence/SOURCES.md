- 987 communautés de communes;
- 230 communautés d'agglomération;
- 14 communautés urbaines;
- 21 métropoles.

# Evidence Hierarchy

Wenergy prioritises:

1. authoritative public evidence;
2. actor-confirmed evidence;
3. expert-validated evidence;
4. transparent deterministic/statistical estimates;
5. inference;
6. explicit assumptions.

---

# Source Integrity

A source is not equivalent to an interpretation.

Wenergy documentation should clearly distinguish:

> **EXTERNAL FACT**

from:

> **WENERGY INTERPRETATION**

and:

> **MODELLED BUSINESS ASSUMPTION**
