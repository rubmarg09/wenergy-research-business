# Wenergy — Claims & Guardrails

This file defines the main interpretation boundaries used throughout Wenergy documentation and product design.

---

# Core Guardrails

> **SCREENING ≠ FEASIBILITY**

> **RELATIONSHIP ≠ OWNERSHIP**

> **DEMAND CONTEXT ≠ METERED DEMAND**

> **CONSTRAINT ≠ AUTOMATIC EXCLUSION OR AUTHORISATION**

> **STRAIGHT-LINE DISTANCE ≠ NETWORK ROUTE**

> **PRIORITY ≠ SUCCESS PROBABILITY / ROI**

> **MAPPED ACTOR ≠ WILLING PARTICIPANT**

> **RESOURCE POTENTIAL ≠ GUARANTEED ENERGY**

> **MISSING EVIDENCE ≠ INVENT DATA**

> **INTEREST ≠ TECHNICAL PRIORITY / VALIDITY**

> **LLM OUTPUT ≠ OBSERVED FACT**

> **POTENTIAL ≠ ACHIEVED IMPACT**

---

# Claim Classes

## POC FACT

Directly observed in the Wenergy POC.

## EXTERNAL FACT

Supported by an authoritative external source.

## MODELLED SCENARIO

Quantitative business or impact planning model.

## STRATEGIC HYPOTHESIS

Requires validation.

## FUTURE TARGET

A goal, not current performance.

---

# What Wenergy Currently Does Not Claim

Wenergy does not currently claim that:

- 32,628 opportunities are feasible;
- all opportunities should advance;
- all mapped actors want to participate;
- mapped relationships prove ownership;
- 25,234.248 MWh/year has been generated by Wenergy;
- verified CO2 savings have been achieved;
- Foix has purchased Wenergy;
- local stakeholder discussions represent endorsement;
- SDE09 is a formal partner;
- willingness to pay is validated;
- pricing is market-proven;
- TAM is observed revenue;
- Wenergy replaces consultants;
- Wenergy replaces engineers;
- Wenergy replaces public energy tools;
- AI should autonomously make public investment decisions.

# Commercial Claims Boundary

Current pilot pricing:

- €7.5k–€15k HT;
- central assumption ~€11k HT.

Recurring licence scenarios:

- ~€19k/year Standard EPCI;
- ~€24k/year Mature EPCI;
- ~€36k/year Advanced Agglomeration;
- ~€55k/year Multi-territory.

These are planning assumptions.

---

# Market Claims Boundary

TAM / SAM / SOM are modelled from:

> **addressable institutional organisations × assumed contract values**

They are not calculated from total climate investment or total energy-system CAPEX.

---

# Environmental Claims Boundary

Screening opportunities should never be converted automatically into claims about:

- installed capacity;
- generated energy;
- carbon savings.

Physical impact requires implementation and measurement.

---

# Stakeholder Claims Boundary

Early discussions with territorial stakeholders are treated as:

> **QUALITATIVE DISCOVERY**

not:

- endorsement;
- commercial commitment;
- procurement;
- partnership.

---

# Public Repository Rule

Public materials should contain:

- no private stakeholder data;
- no secrets;
- no API keys;
- no private credentials;
- no misleading screenshots;
- no generated image presented as real product evidence.

Actual product screenshots must be identified as real captures.
