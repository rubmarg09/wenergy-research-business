# Current Interpretation

The current POC demonstrates:

- real territorial data integration;
- multi-energy architecture;
- actor mapping;
- opportunity generation;
- constraint-context enrichment;
- Project Lens generation;
- evidence-aware workflow.

It does not yet demonstrate:

- paid customer validation;
- renewal;
- professional feasibility success;
- implemented energy projects;
- measured environmental impact.

---

# Current Product Boundary

> **SCREENING ≠ FEASIBILITY**

Wenergy's current role is to prepare a stronger professional investigation pipeline.

---

# Current POC Maturity

Primary product maturity:

> **M0–M3**

Physical implementation and measured impact remain downstream.
