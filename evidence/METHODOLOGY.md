# Wenergy — Methodology

## Purpose

Wenergy is an early-stage territorial energy screening and orchestration system.

It is designed to identify and structure candidate project relationships for deeper professional investigation.

It is not a feasibility engine.

---

# Core Methodological Principle

> **SCREENING ≠ FEASIBILITY**

Wenergy primarily operates across:

- M0 — Candidate
- M1 — Evidence-supported
- M2 — Context-enriched
- M3 — Selected for professional investigation

Qualified professionals then carry projects into:

- M4 — Professional feasibility
- M5 — Funded / permitted / procured
- M6 — Implemented
- M7 — Measured

---

# Evidence Classes

Wenergy distinguishes:

- OBSERVED
- ESTIMATED
- INFERRED
- ACTOR-CONFIRMED
- EXPERT-VALIDATED
- ASSUMED

---

# Core Workflow

> **EXPLORE → CONNECT → MATCH → UNDERSTAND → DECIDE → ENGAGE → ACT → FOLLOW → LEARN → MEASURE**

---

A Project Lens can contain:

- resource;

- infrastructure context;

- evidence class;
- uncertainty;

- next action.

---

# Methodological Boundaries

## Ownership

> **RELATIONSHIP ≠ OWNERSHIP**

Mapped proximity or association does not prove ownership or control.

## Demand

> **DEMAND CONTEXT ≠ METERED DEMAND**

Contextual or modelled demand must not be presented as actual metered consumption.

## Spatial Relationships

> **STRAIGHT-LINE DISTANCE ≠ NETWORK ROUTE**

Physical connection feasibility requires professional network analysis.

## Constraints

> **CONSTRAINT ≠ AUTOMATIC EXCLUSION OR AUTHORISATION**

Mapped constraints provide screening context, not legal permission.

## Priority

> **PRIORITY ≠ SUCCESS PROBABILITY / ROI**

Priority represents relative investigation value under current evidence.

## Actors

> **MAPPED ACTOR ≠ WILLING PARTICIPANT**

Participation must be confirmed.

## Energy Potential

> **RESOURCE POTENTIAL ≠ GUARANTEED ENERGY**

Potential must not be reported as delivered generation.

---

# Low-Data Methodology

Low-data mode uses:

- reliable available data;
- transparent estimates;
- statistical ranges;
- proxies;
- confidence levels;
- local evidence collection;
- professional validation.

Core rule:

> **LESS DATA SHOULD MEAN LESS CERTAINTY — NOT ZERO PRODUCT.**

Missing evidence should not be fabricated.

---

# AI Methodology

LLMs may assist with:

- extraction;
- explanation;
- translation;
- summarisation;
- structuring;
- validation questions.

Core rule:

> **LLM OUTPUT ≠ OBSERVED FACT**

High-volume numerical screening should prefer:

- deterministic algorithms;
- geospatial computation;
- published factors;
- statistical methods.

---

# Impact Methodology

Impact should progress through:

1. Potential
2. Estimated
3. Expert-validated
4. Measured

Core rule:

> **POTENTIAL ≠ ACHIEVED IMPACT**

---

# Commercial Methodology

Financial figures are planning scenarios.

They are not observed revenue.

Pricing, CAC, retention, customer count, margins and growth require real commercial validation.

---

# Replication Methodology

The second-territory target is:

> **≥70% workflow reuse**

This is a future validation target.

It is not currently proven.
