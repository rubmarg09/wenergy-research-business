# Wenergy

## Territorial Energy Intelligence & Orchestration

**CONNECT • MATCH • ACT**

> Turning fragmented local energy potential into actionable community projects.

Wenergy is a multi-energy territorial intelligence and orchestration platform designed to help communities understand which local clean-energy opportunities deserve professional investigation first — and why.

It connects:

**local energy needs + renewable and recoverable resources + infrastructure + actors + constraints + evidence**

into structured **Project Lenses** that can be understood, prioritised, validated and progressively advanced toward real-world action.

Wenergy does **not** replace energy engineers, consultants, ALECs, energy syndicates, network operators, feasibility studies, public governance or installers.

It is designed to connect them.

# The problem

Territories increasingly have access to energy data, renewable-resource maps, public datasets, consultants and specialist tools.

Yet data availability does not automatically create project delivery capacity.

A small or resource-constrained territory may still need to answer:

- Which local energy opportunities actually deserve attention?
- Which resource could serve which local need?
- Which actors need to cooperate?
- Which environmental or planning constraints matter?
- Which evidence is observed and which is estimated?
- Which candidate should receive scarce engineering budget first?
- Which specialist should be engaged next?
- How can residents understand what is being considered?
- What happened to the project after feasibility?

Wenergy calls this the **Territorial Energy Execution-Intelligence Gap**.

# Pilot territory

The first Proof of Concept is being developed for **Foix / Pays Foix-Varilhes in Ariège, France**.

The pilot environment is particularly useful because it combines:

- a small urban centre;
- rural communes;
- public buildings;
- agriculture;
- businesses;
- renewable and recoverable-energy resources;
- environmental constraints;
- limited local public-sector capacity compared with large metropolitan authorities.

The broader Pays Foix-Varilhes territory includes **42 communes**, approximately **33,679 inhabitants** and around **444 km²**.

The pilot is intended to test whether Wenergy can help a territory move from a very large opportunity universe toward a much smaller set of evidence-backed candidates deserving professional investigation.

# Product philosophy

The Wenergy journey is:

**EXPLORE → CONNECT → MATCH → UNDERSTAND → DECIDE → ENGAGE → ACT → FOLLOW → LEARN → MEASURE**

### Explore

Understand the territory through the Home view, map, opportunities, actors and evidence.

### Connect

Create a shared **Data & Energy Fabric** linking fragmented territorial evidence while preserving provenance and uncertainty.

### Match

Use deterministic and mathematical screening to identify plausible relationships between:

**RESOURCE ↔ NEED ↔ ACTORS ↔ INFRASTRUCTURE ↔ CONSTRAINTS**

### Understand

Convert candidate relationships into a structured **Project Lens**.

### Decide

Help territorial users compare, shortlist, deprioritise and prepare candidates for professional investigation.

### Engage

Allow appropriate actors and community members to follow opportunities, contribute information or express governed interest.

**Interest is not technical priority.**

### Act

Connect shortlisted opportunities to the relevant professional, institution, feasibility process, funding path or next validation action.

### Follow

Track the project through an **Opportunity Journey**.

### Learn

Return actor confirmation, professional feasibility and rejection evidence into the Project Lens.

### Measure

Only implemented projects become measured physical and social impact.

# Project Lens

The Project Lens is the core Wenergy product object.

A Lens can bring together:

- resource;
- local energy need;
- project purpose;
- actors;
- infrastructure;
- constraints;
- evidence;
- uncertainty;
- scenario information;
- project maturity;
- next professional action.

The central distinction is:

> **SCREENING ≠ FEASIBILITY**

Wenergy orients and connects.

Qualified professionals validate, engineer and implement.

# Evidence model

Wenergy explicitly distinguishes:

- **OBSERVED**
- **ESTIMATED**
- **INFERRED**
- **ACTOR-CONFIRMED**
- **EXPERT-VALIDATED**
- **ASSUMED**

This allows the platform to show:

**what is known, how it is known, what remains uncertain and what must be validated next.**

Other methodological guardrails include:

- Relationship ≠ ownership
- Demand context ≠ metered demand
- Constraint ≠ automatic exclusion or authorisation
- Straight-line distance ≠ network route
- Priority ≠ success probability
- Mapped actor ≠ willing participant
- Resource potential ≠ guaranteed energy
- Missing evidence ≠ permission to invent data
- Interest ≠ technical validity

# High-data, partial-data and low-data territories

The French POC benefits from a strong public-data environment.

Wenergy is deliberately being designed so that this does not become a prerequisite for access.

The target architecture supports three evidence modes:

### High-data mode

Authoritative datasets and granular observed evidence.

### Partial-data mode

Observed evidence combined with transparent proxies, statistical estimates, ranges and actor confirmation.

### Low-data mode

Minimum available geographic, demographic and local evidence combined with:

- transparent estimation models;
- confidence levels;
- data-gap identification;
- manually collected evidence;
- local actor contribution;
- progressive professional validation.

The principle is:

> **LESS DATA SHOULD MEAN LESS CERTAINTY — NOT ZERO PRODUCT.**

In data-rich territories, Wenergy primarily connects intelligence that already exists.

In data-poor territories, it can also help progressively build that intelligence.

# Community access

Wenergy follows a **B2G2C** model.

The primary paying customer is expected to be a territorial public organisation such as:

- an EPCI / communauté de communes;
- an energy syndicate;
- a selected agglomeration;
- another appropriate territorial organisation.

The territorial licence can then support access for:

- member communes;
- technical teams;
- experts;
- businesses;
- farmers;
- local actors;
- residents.

The long-term objective is for the essential community-facing layer to be available to residents without an individual subscription.

A future optional Premium consumer layer may add personal functionality, but payment must not influence public project ranking.

Wenergy operates inside an existing ecosystem.

Important adjacent / alternative solutions include:

- territorial transition platforms such as TerriSTORY;
- specialist opportunity tools such as EnRezo;
- national renewable-energy planning portals;
- Enedis, INSEE, IGN and ADEME data infrastructure;
- GIS / spreadsheets / internal territorial tools;
- ALECs and energy syndicates;
- engineering firms and consultants;
- energy companies and project developers;
- community consultation tools.

Wenergy does not claim those solutions are unnecessary.

Its intended white space is the orchestration layer between them.

> **DATA EXISTS. TOOLS EXIST. EXPERTS EXIST. ACTORS EXIST.  
> THEY DO NOT AUTOMATICALLY BECOME A COHERENT PROJECT PIPELINE.**

Wenergy attempts to connect that pipeline.

# Environmental design

Wenergy is software-first and does not require proprietary Wenergy energy hardware.

Environmental design principles include:

- deterministic-first computation;
- selective and right-sized AI;
- no mandatory proprietary sensor network;
- reuse of authoritative public data;
- reusable connectors;
- incremental refresh;
- shared territorial infrastructure;
- low-compute operation;
- multi-energy rather than technology-first screening;
- explicit environmental constraint context;
- lifecycle-aware project investigation;
- measured impact rather than theoretical impact inflation.

The objective is not to maximise project quantity.

It is to improve the quality of decisions made before real materials, capital, land and professional effort are committed.

# Social impact

The primary social-impact area is:

**Inclusive territorial energy access, community empowerment and local energy decision capacity.**

The Foix pilot also contains a real affordability context.

Official INSEE evidence used in the business research indicates approximately:

- **24.0% poverty rate in Foix commune**
- **16.1% poverty rate across the wider EPCI**

Wenergy does not claim that software solves poverty.

The relevance is that financially constrained households and public institutions have less capacity to absorb inefficient energy spending or repeated analytical work.

The platform aims to:

- mutualise territorial analytical capacity;
- improve access for small communes;
- make local project information understandable to residents;
- connect actors to professional expertise;
- improve allocation of scarce study budgets;
- make community participation possible without making popularity a technical score.

# Monitoring and evaluation

Wenergy separates success into:

1. Product and data performance
2. Decision efficiency
3. User/community value
4. Project progression
5. Implemented and measured impact

Primary proposed pilot metric:

## Decision-to-Action Efficiency

Percentage reduction in human time and effort required to move from fragmented territorial evidence to an evidence-backed shortlist ready for professional investigation.

Additional pilot indicators include:

- Time to First Useful Project Lens
- Evidence traceability rate
- Expert usefulness
- Territorial-user usefulness
- Next-action completeness
- Qualified Project Lenses entering professional investigation
- Decisions changed / accelerated / deprioritised
- Actor engagement
- Community understanding
- Small-commune access

Only implemented projects progress to realised energy, financial, environmental and social outcomes.

**screening intelligence**
