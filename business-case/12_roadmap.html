Chapter 12 / 12

Prove locally. Repeat responsibly. Scale only when the evidence earns it.

Wenergy's roadmap is deliberately evidence-gated. The priority is not to claim global scale early, but to prove that the territorial intelligence and orchestration workflow creates real value, can be repeated and can support a sustainable business.

Foix First Second Territory Productisation Partner Scale Selective International
North Star

Become the shared territorial intelligence layer that helps communities identify, understand, prioritise and advance stronger local energy projects.

Not by replacing engineering, consultants, public tools or local institutions — but by helping those systems work together around a common, evidence-backed project pipeline.

Strategic Sequence

Six stages — each earned by the one before it.

1 Functional POC Build and test the territorial screening architecture.
2 Foix Pilot Prove usefulness, decision impact and willingness to pay.
3 Second Territory Test reuse and reduce bespoke delivery.
4 Productise Standardise adapters, workflows and recurring value.
5 Partner Scale Use SDE/ALEC/institutional channels to extend reach.
6 Selective Expansion Scale nationally, then adapt internationally where evidence supports it.

What Must Be Proven

The next stage depends on six truths.

1 — Decision Value Wenergy materially improves which opportunities receive attention.
2 — Willingness to Pay Public buyers value the workflow enough to fund it sustainably.
3 — Repeatability New territories reuse the core product instead of requiring a rebuild.
4 — Recurring Need Project intelligence remains useful after the first screening cycle.
5 — Trust Users understand and accept evidence, uncertainty and governance boundaries.
6 — Ecosystem Fit Wenergy strengthens existing public/professional workflows rather than creating duplication.

Final Thesis

The energy transition does not only need more technologies. It needs better ways to see how local resources, needs, people and professional capabilities fit together.

Wenergy is being built to answer that coordination problem: connect fragmented territorial evidence, identify stronger project relationships, explain uncertainty, help communities and public teams decide what deserves attention and move the strongest opportunities toward qualified action.

The ambition is not to make every project possible. It is to make better projects easier to find, understand and advance.

THE TERRITORY SHOULD HELP DETERMINE THE TECHNOLOGY — NOT THE TECHNOLOGY DETERMINE THE TERRITORY.
