THE TERRITORY SHOULD HELP DETERMINE THE TECHNOLOGY — NOT THE TECHNOLOGY DETERMINE THE TERRITORY.
Waste Heat Example

A resource becomes valuable only when the relationship works.

01 — Source Identify recoverable heat.
02 — Demand Find compatible nearby need.
03 — Infrastructure Evaluate spatial/network context.
04 — Actors Connect source and demand operators.
05 — Feasibility Qualified professionals validate the real project.
A waste-heat source alone is not a project. A demand point alone is not a project. Environmental value emerges from a technically and economically viable relationship.
Circularity

Waste can become a resource — but energy recovery is not automatically the best answer.

1 — Avoid Prevent unnecessary resource use where possible.
2 — Reuse Preserve direct utility before conversion.
3 — Material Recovery Recycle valuable materials where appropriate.
4 — Energy Recovery Use residual energy where technically justified.
5 — Disposal Last resort.
ENERGY RECOVERY SHOULD NOT UNDERMINE HIGHER-VALUE CIRCULAR USES.

Anti-Greenwashing Framework

Every impact value should reveal its maturity.

POTENTIAL Screening suggests an opportunity may exist.
ESTIMATED Transparent model calculates a possible outcome.
EXPERT-VALIDATED Qualified professional confirms methodology / feasibility evidence.
MEASURED Operating evidence records the actual outcome.
POTENTIAL ≠ ACHIEVED IMPACT Wenergy should never convert an early screening estimate directly into a headline carbon or energy claim.
