Chapter 05 / 12

Public value and commercial sustainability can reinforce each other.

Wenergy's preferred model is B2G2C: a territorial public organisation pays for shared project-intelligence infrastructure, while the value extends across communes, professionals, businesses, farmers and communities.

B2G2C Paid Pilot Entry Recurring Territorial Licence Vendor Neutral
Business Model

One buyer. Multiple users. Shared territorial value.

Wenergy is designed so the party with the institutional mandate and purchasing capacity can fund the infrastructure, without requiring every beneficiary to become an individual paying customer.

Pays

Territorial Buyer

EPCI, energy syndicate or other territorial public organisation funds the pilot and/or annual licence.

→
Uses

Territorial & Professional Users

Technical teams, energy leads, ALECs, experts and partner organisations use deeper project-intelligence capabilities.

→
Benefits

Communes & Community

Smaller communes, businesses, farmers and residents gain clearer access to local energy-project information and participation pathways.

TERRITORY PAYS → SHARED LOCAL CAPABILITY → BROADER COMMUNITY ACCESS
Value Exchange

The model only works if every side gets meaningful value.

Territory Better prioritisation, less fragmented analysis, project memory and clearer professional handoff.
Professional Ecosystem Better-prepared candidate projects and less low-value discovery work.
Local Actors Clearer project context, visible evidence gaps and more structured engagement.
Community Better understanding of local opportunities without requiring premium access to core territorial information.
Commercial Entry

Start with a bounded paid pilot.

The first product is not a massive transformation programme. It is a focused Territorial Opportunity Prioritisation Pilot designed to prove decision value before asking the customer to commit to recurring use.

Entry Product

Territorial Opportunity Prioritisation Pilot

Proposed scope can include:

  • territorial opportunity overview;
  • multi-energy screening;
  • Project Lens longlist;
  • priority shortlist;
  • data-gap register;
  • actor map;
  • professional handoff plan;
  • pilot value report.

Revenue Architecture

Recurring territorial SaaS at the core.

Core Revenue

Annual Territorial Licence

The long-term business depends on recurring value:

  • new evidence refreshes;
  • new project opportunities;
  • project maturity tracking;
  • actor updates;
  • portfolio memory;
  • new pathways;
  • community/project communication.

SaaS sustainability requires recurring decision value.

Paid Pilot Initial validation and onboarding revenue.
Expansion Modules Additional pathways, territories or advanced capabilities.
Limited Services Bounded onboarding or implementation support without becoming a consulting-heavy business.
Future Premium Optional individual features only if they do not undermine equitable access to core community information.
Public Procurement

The product must be easy enough to buy.

Public-sector fit depends not only on product value but on a procurement pathway that is proportionate, documented and understandable.

1 Discovery Confirm pain and buyer.
2 Scope Define bounded pilot.
3 Budget Confirm internal funding path.
4 Procurement Follow applicable public rules.
5 Pilot Deliver measurable value.
6 Renew Convert to recurring licence.
Boundary: this business case deliberately avoids hardcoding legal procurement thresholds without current legal verification. Actual procurement must follow the rules applicable to the buyer and contract.
