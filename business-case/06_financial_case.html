Funding Logic

Match capital to evidence milestones.

Risk

Public-sector sales cycles may be slow.

Procurement, budget alignment and institutional decision-making can delay revenue even when customer interest exists.

→
Response

Milestone-based financing.

Core Assumptions

The model is only as strong as the assumptions underneath it.

Pricing Pilot and annual licence assumptions require real customer validation.
Conversion Paid pilot must convert into recurring use.
Retention Wenergy must provide ongoing value beyond initial territory screening.
Replication Deployment must become substantially reusable.
Delivery Cost Human onboarding effort must decline over time.
Sales Cycle Public procurement timing must remain compatible with runway.
THE FINANCIAL MODEL DOES NOT NEED WENERGY TO DOMINATE FRANCE. The Base scenario reaches €926k ending ARR with 35 active customers — a small share of the French institutional buyer universe.
