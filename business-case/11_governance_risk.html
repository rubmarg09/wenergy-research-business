Chapter 11 / 12

Decision intelligence only creates value if users can trust how the decision was formed.

Wenergy's governance model is built around explicit evidence provenance, commercial neutrality, visible uncertainty, professional role boundaries, secure handling of information and clear escalation when confidence is insufficient.

Governance Principles

Five rules protect the integrity of the platform.

01 Evidence Before Assertion Important claims should retain source, date, evidence class and uncertainty.
02 Commercial Neutrality Payment must not buy technical priority.
03 Professional Boundaries Screening does not replace feasibility, permitting or engineering.
04 Human Accountability High-stakes progression requires human and professional review.
05 Visible Uncertainty Missing data must reduce confidence instead of generating artificial precision.
06 Data Minimisation Collect only information required for the legitimate product purpose.
07 Auditability Priority outputs should be reconstructable from their evidence and rules.
08 Correction Actor or expert evidence can improve Project Lenses over time.
Commercial Neutrality

Access can be monetised. Evidence cannot be bought.

Allowed

Customers may pay for capabilities.

  • licence access;
  • additional users;
  • territorial coverage;
  • advanced modules;
  • support;
  • integration services.
COMMERCIAL FIREWALL
Prohibited

Customers must not pay to alter technical truth.

  • boost a technology;
  • promote a supplier;
  • hide a constraint;
  • change evidence class;
  • fabricate certainty;
  • suppress conflicting data.
PAYMENT MAY PURCHASE ACCESS TO CAPABILITIES. PAYMENT MUST NOT PURCHASE TECHNICAL PRIORITY.
Evidence Governance

Every material input needs a status.

OBSERVED Directly supported by source evidence.
ESTIMATED Calculated using transparent methodology.
INFERRED Derived relationship or classification.
ACTOR-CONFIRMED Confirmed by relevant local actor.
EXPERT-VALIDATED Reviewed by qualified professional.
ASSUMED Explicit planning assumption.
AN OUTPUT SHOULD NEVER LOOK MORE CERTAIN THAN ITS UNDERLYING EVIDENCE.
Data Governance

Public data does not eliminate governance responsibility.

Purpose Limitation Use information only for defined territorial energy-intelligence purposes.
Data Minimisation Avoid collecting unnecessary personal or sensitive information.
Provenance Preserve source, retrieval date and transformation history.
Freshness Track whether important datasets are stale or current.
Correction Support updates when actors or experts identify errors.
Role-Based Access Not every user needs every data layer.
Retention Define how long private or operational data should remain.
Export / Portability Public buyers should retain usable project evidence.
AI Governance

AI can assist explanation. It must not become the source of truth.

Appropriate

Assist the evidence workflow

Extract structured fields from documents.
Summarise or translate source material.
Explain Project Lenses in accessible language.
Categorise evidence for human review.
Suggest missing validation questions.
Not Acceptable

Do not let AI manufacture evidence

Invent consumption or production values.
Fabricate stakeholder willingness.
Convert assumptions into observed facts.
Override professional validation.
Hide conflicting or missing evidence.
LLM OUTPUT ≠ OBSERVED FACT
Security Posture

Start simple — but design for institutional trust.

Secrets Management No API keys, passwords or private credentials in public repositories.
Least Privilege Restrict administrative and sensitive functions by role.
Dependency Hygiene Track and patch vulnerable software dependencies.
Secure Configuration Separate local/development settings from production configuration.
Audit Logs Future production deployments should record important changes.
Backups Protect project evidence and configuration from accidental loss.
Data Segregation Keep private customer information separated from public evidence.
Incident Process Define response and notification procedures before scale.
These controls describe the intended production governance direction. The current public POC should not be represented as a certified production security environment.

Risk Register

The strongest risks are product, market and trust risks — not only technical risks.

Risk Probability Impact Mitigation Evidence Gate
Customers do not find sufficient incremental value High High Paid pilot with explicit usefulness and decision-change KPIs. Do not scale GTM without pilot proof.
Public tools close the white space Medium High Integrate public tools and focus on workflow/orchestration rather than duplicate features. Reassess differentiation annually.
Territory onboarding remains too bespoke High High Adapter architecture + second-territory reuse target. ≥70% workflow reuse target.
Screening errors undermine trust Medium High Evidence classes, provenance, review and clear screening boundary. ≥90% traceability target.
False precision in low-data environments Medium High Confidence degradation, ranges and explicit data-gap Lens. No unsupported observed claim.
AI hallucination affects project evidence Medium High LLM never treated as observed source; deterministic-first architecture. Human review before high-stakes use.
Public procurement cycle exceeds runway High High Lean staffing, paid pilot entry and partner-channel GTM. Track sales-cycle evidence before hiring.
Commercial influence damages neutrality Low High Technical-priority firewall and transparent scoring/evidence rules. No pay-to-rank model.
Private data leaks into public repository Medium High Publication audit, separate public demo data and secret scanning. Zero secrets before release.
Founder dependence remains too high High Medium Document workflows, adapters, methodology and repeatable onboarding. Delivery-hour decline by territory.
Escalation Model

When confidence falls, the workflow should slow down — not pretend harder.

1 — Detect Evidence gap, conflict or low-confidence condition.
2 — Flag Make the uncertainty visible in the Project Lens.
3 — Collect Request local or authoritative evidence.
4 — Review Human / professional validation if needed.
5 — Update Recalculate confidence and next action.
Kill Criteria

Governance also means knowing when not to proceed.

No Customer Value Pilot users do not find the workflow materially useful.
No Willingness to Pay Public buyers consistently value the product below sustainable delivery cost.
No Replication Every territory requires a bespoke rebuild.
No Defensible Boundary Existing free tools and professional services solve the problem sufficiently.
Unmanageable Data Risk The product cannot maintain acceptable evidence quality or privacy.
Neutrality Compromised Commercial incentives require pay-to-rank behaviour.
AI Becomes Source of Truth The system cannot maintain evidence-grounded outputs.
Unsustainable Delivery Services effort prevents a scalable software model.
A STRONG GOVERNANCE MODEL MUST ALLOW WENERGY TO SAY: “THIS SHOULD NOT PROCEED.”
Governance KPIs

≥90% Priority-output evidence traceability target.
100% Priority Project Lenses with evidence class.
0 Pay-to-rank pathways tolerated.
0 Secrets allowed in public repositories.
100% High-risk AI outputs subject to review.
Tracked Data freshness for material sources.
Tracked Corrections and evidence updates.
Explicit Role boundary for every maturity stage.
TRUST IS PART OF THE PRODUCT. Wenergy's value depends not only on surfacing promising opportunities, but on making it clear what is known, what is uncertain, who validated it, what Wenergy is responsible for and where qualified professionals must take over.
