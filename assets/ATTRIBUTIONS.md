## Attribution Principle

Wenergy aims to distinguish clearly between:

- original Wenergy assets;
- public institutional information;
- third-party copyrighted or trademarked materials;
- Creative Commons / openly licensed assets.

If an attribution or licence issue is identified, the affected asset should
be corrected or removed before public release.
