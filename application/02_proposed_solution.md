# Wenergy — Go Green Challenge 2026

## Application Q02 — Proposed Solution

### What is your proposed solution?

**Wenergy is a Territorial Energy Intelligence & Orchestration platform that connects local energy needs, renewable and recoverable resources, infrastructure, actors and constraints to identify, explain, prioritise and help advance local clean-energy projects.**

Its purpose is not to replace consultants, engineers, energy agencies or public tools.

Its role is to:

> **ORIENT, CONNECT AND ENABLE ACTION.**

Qualified professionals remain responsible for detailed feasibility, engineering and implementation.

---

## CONNECT • MATCH • ACT

### CONNECT

Wenergy connects fragmented territorial evidence:

- energy resources;
- demand;
- actors;
- buildings;
- infrastructure;
- environmental constraints;
- planning context;
- public datasets;
- professional evidence.

### MATCH

The platform identifies plausible project relationships across multiple pathways, including:

- solar;
- waste heat;
- biomass;
- geothermal;
- hydro;
- wind;
- agricultural biogas;
- municipal waste / recoverable energy.

### ACT

Each stronger opportunity becomes a **Project Lens**.

A Project Lens can contain:

- resource;
- need;
- actors;
- infrastructure context;
- constraint context;
- evidence provenance;
- confidence;
- uncertainty;
- project maturity;
- next action.

This helps users understand not only what may be possible, but what must happen next.

## Evidence Model

Wenergy distinguishes:

- OBSERVED;
- ESTIMATED;
- INFERRED;
- ACTOR-CONFIRMED;
- EXPERT-VALIDATED;
- ASSUMED.

The rule is:

> **MISSING EVIDENCE SHOULD REDUCE CERTAINTY — NOT CREATE FALSE PRECISION.**

---

## Low-Data Mode

For territories with incomplete data, Wenergy is designed around:

- transparent proxies;
- scenario ranges;
- confidence levels;
- Data-Gap Lenses;
- local actor evidence;
- professional validation.

Core principle:

> **LESS DATA SHOULD MEAN LESS CERTAINTY — NOT ZERO PRODUCT.**

---

## Business Model

The proposed model is B2G2C.

A territorial public buyer pays for a shared licence, while core community information can remain accessible to local users.

The initial commercial entry point is a:

> **Territorial Opportunity Prioritisation Pilot**

Current pricing hypothesis:

- €7.5k–€15k HT pilot;
- central pilot assumption around €11k.

These prices are assumptions to validate.

---

## Primary Efficiency Metric

Wenergy's primary proposed operational metric is:

> **Decision-to-Action Efficiency**

This measures how much the platform reduces the human time and effort required to move from fragmented territorial evidence to an actionable professional shortlist.

---

## Core Product Boundary

> **SCREENING ≠ FEASIBILITY**

> **RELATIONSHIP ≠ OWNERSHIP**

> **PRIORITY ≠ SUCCESS PROBABILITY**

> **LLM OUTPUT ≠ OBSERVED FACT**

Wenergy prepares stronger projects for professional investigation.

It does not replace professional responsibility.
